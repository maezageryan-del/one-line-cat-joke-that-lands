## The joke

> My cat claimed me as a dependent on her taxes: I provide the food, the shelter, the emotional support, and the litter duty.

123 characters, one line. The joke inverts the usual roles: the cat is the taxpayer and the human is the dependent — and the itemised list of what the human provides proves her right, with the litter duty landing last as the most degrading item on the bill.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

## Why this line

The field's 557 entries cluster on six tired angles — 3 a.m. zoomies, the performance review, knocking things off the table, the expensive bed the cat won't sleep in, the keyboard promotion, the gravity audit. None of them gives the cat a financial power over the human. This one does: a cat who files taxes, claims her human as a dependent, and bills her in food, shelter, emotional support — and litter duty.
